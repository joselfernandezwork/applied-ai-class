New Category — AI That Operates Your Computer

AI tools that control your computer

A new generation of AI doesn't just chat — it actually clicks, types, scrolls, and runs apps on your machine. These "computer-use" agents can book travel, fill spreadsheets, sort email, or move files for you. They are powerful, but you are giving an AI real keyboard-and-mouse control, so always supervise them, run them in a sandbox where possible, and never give them access to passwords or accounts you cannot afford to lose.

⚠️ Safety first. Treat a computer-use agent like an intern with admin access: limit what it can see, watch what it does, and stop it if it goes off-script. Prefer a virtual machine, browser profile, or work-only account. Disable autofill for sensitive logins, and never let it act on your real banking, healthcare, or HR systems unattended.

OpenClaw
Open desktop agent that clicks for you

What it is: An open computer-use agent that takes screenshots of your desktop, reasons about what's on screen, and then moves the mouse, types, and clicks to complete the task you described in plain English.

Good for: Repetitive desktop chores — filling forms, tagging files, scraping a UI that has no API, automating internal apps that nobody wants to integrate.

  • 1Go to openclaw.ai and create an account (or download the app)
  • 2Install the desktop client and grant screen-recording + accessibility permissions
  • 3Connect a model provider key (e.g. Claude or GPT) when prompted
  • 4Pick "New Task" → describe the goal: "Open Excel, sort sheet by column B, save"
  • 5Watch the live preview and click Pause/Stop the moment it does something unexpected

Claude Dispatch
Send tasks from your phone to your computer · Anthropic

What it is: Anthropic's "dispatch" flow built on top of Claude's computer-use tool. From the Claude mobile app you hand off a task — "clean up my downloads folder", "rebook this flight", "draft replies to today's email" — and Claude carries it out on your desktop using its own keyboard and mouse, then reports back.

Good for: Long-running, away-from-keyboard work: research while you commute, file cleanup overnight, batch operations across desktop apps you can't easily script.

  • 1Install the Claude desktop app on the machine you want it to drive (claude.ai → Download)
  • 2Sign in to the same Claude account on the mobile app
  • 3Enable computer use / Dispatch in Settings on desktop and grant the OS permissions it requests
  • 4From mobile, start a chat → "Dispatch this to my computer" → describe the task in plain English
  • 5Leave the desktop unlocked; check progress on your phone and approve or interrupt as it works

Perplexity Computer
Personal AI assistant for your Mac · Perplexity

What it is: Perplexity's desktop assistant that goes beyond search — it reads what's on your screen, answers questions in context, and can take actions for you (browse the web, fill forms, pull info between apps). Currently rolling out on Mac.

Good for: Research-heavy desk work — comparing options across tabs, summarising what's on screen, kicking off small "go do this for me" tasks without leaving your current app.

  • 1Go to perplexity.ai and download the Mac app (sign in with your Perplexity account)
  • 2Grant screen-recording and accessibility permissions in System Settings → Privacy & Security
  • 3Press the global hotkey to summon the assistant on top of any app
  • 4Ask about what's on screen, or say "go do X" — e.g. "find three flights cheaper than this one"
  • 5Review each action it proposes before approving; keep sensitive accounts logged out in that browser
